What it does

Everything you need between casting and curtain-up

Built around the way actors actually learn lines — not the way software usually wants you to.

Import any script

Photograph a printed page, bring in a PDF or Word document, drop in plain text, or type it in by hand. OffBook works out who says what.

Hide your lines

Blank out your character's lines and cue yourself through the scene, revealing each one only when you've had a proper go at it.

Rehearse out loud

Speak your lines and have them checked against the script with voice recognition, so you're learning them the way you'll perform them.

Confidence scoring

Every run is scored, so you can see at a glance which scenes are solid and which ones still need another pass before you're in the room.

Productions & characters

Keep every show you're in side by side, each with its own cast list, scenes and the character you're actually playing.

Share with the cast

Send a prepared script to the rest of the company as an .offbook file, so nobody has to type the same scene twice.

How it works

Script in your hand to off book in three moves

Get the script in

Point your camera at the page, or import the PDF the director emailed you. OffBook reads it, splits it into lines, and asks which character is yours.

Run the scene

Your lines are hidden; everyone else's are there to cue you. Say yours aloud and OffBook checks them, or tap to reveal when you're stuck.

Watch it stick

Each run leaves a confidence score behind. Come back tomorrow, run it again, and see the line climb — that's the bit that tells you you're ready.

Confidence scoring

Stop guessing whether you know it

"I think I'm fine on Act Two" is not a plan. OffBook scores every rehearsal run and keeps the history, so the question stops being a feeling and starts being a number you can act on.

Weak scenes surface themselves. You spend your twenty minutes on the pages that actually need it, instead of running the opening you already nailed a week ago.
