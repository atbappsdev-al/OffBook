What you get

The whole rehearsal loop, in your pocket

Import any script

Hide your lines

Before you start

OffBook asks for two optional permissions, and only when you use the feature that needs one:

Camera — to photograph a printed script.

Microphone — to rehearse lines out loud.

No account required

There's nothing to sign up for. No ads, no tracking, and your productions and rehearsal history stay on your device — just you and your lines.
